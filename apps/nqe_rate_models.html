Crossover temperature Tc = ħ|ω‡|/2πkB (set by the barrier frequency) is where the reaction stops behaving classically. Above it, passage over the barrier top dominates and a simple correction like Wigner's is a good approximation. Below it, the dominant path tunnels well beneath the top — Wigner then badly underestimates the rate, and only the full Eckart transmission captures it. It only appears as the dashed red line when it falls inside the T range set below — the "Above the crossover" preset keeps Tc off the bottom of the plot on purpose, since every plotted temperature is meant to sit above it.
