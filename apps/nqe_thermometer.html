Quantum thermometer Zero-point delocalization and the classical limit

Zero-point spread (T → 0)
—

The size of the ring is how far the particle is spread out. A classical particle has no spread at all. Here the spread grows as the temperature falls and is largest at T = 0: the zero-point spread, which never goes away. Heavier isotopes are spread out less.
