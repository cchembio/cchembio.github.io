A ring of 32 beads represents the imaginary-time tunneling path. Its action is minimized by plain steepest descent — no dedicated saddle search — which only finds the true instanton because the ring starts, and stays, exactly mirror-symmetric between the two wells.

The bulge height (skew) bends the minimum-energy path without moving the saddle or either minimum — both always sit exactly on that curve, whatever the skew.
