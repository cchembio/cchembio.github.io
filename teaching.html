L² Inner Product Demo

Mathematics for Physical Chemistry — function spaces & orthogonality

Visualises how the finite-dimensional dot product generalises to the L² inner product ⟨f, g⟩ = ∫ f(x)g(x) dx via midpoint Riemann sums. Adjust the number of sample points N and toggle the Δx weighting to see convergence — or divergence — in real time. Includes five curated function pairs covering orthogonality, Fourier modes, and the role of domain symmetry.
