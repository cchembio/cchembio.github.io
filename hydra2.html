HyDRA II — Blind Prediction Results

OH-stretch red-shifts and optimised geometries for water–acceptor hydrogen-bonded complexes

How to use this page: Select your anonymous participant code from the dropdown below to highlight your predictions in both visualisations. The code is the 6-character identifier you received at registration.

Predicted Frequency Shifts

Deviation of each predicted OH-stretch red-shift from the experimental reference (cm−1). Participants compared to the deperturbed value use that as their reference; those compared to the raw value use the raw frequency. Both are computed relative to the 3657 cm−1 water monomer. The shaded bands show the global deperturbed uncertainty (asymmetric) around zero. Horizontal bars mark the median deviation; error bars are reported prediction uncertainties. Test molecules are on the left; bonus molecules on the right.

Prediction Scores

RMSD and MAE are computed against the respective experimental reference (raw or deperturbed) using 3657 cm−1 as the monomer reference. Log-likelihood and CRPS are probabilistic scores that account for reported uncertainties. Select a participant code to highlight their row.

Aligned Molecular Structures

Optimised geometries of the water–acceptor complexes from all participating groups, superimposed by Kabsch alignment on the three water atoms. Select a molecule to display all submitted structures simultaneously as wireframes.
